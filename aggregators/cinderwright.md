Cinderwright indexes AI agent payment services across three protocols: x402 (USDC on Base), L402 (Lightning over HTTP), and MPP (Stripe/Tempo). The discovery endpoint is free. Services are graded A through F based on weekly canary testing measuring uptime, response time, and payment flow reliability.

Agents can search the index at no cost via `GET /discover?q=keywords` and use the payment proxy to call any indexed service without implementing x402 signing directly.
